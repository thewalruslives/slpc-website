A Swedish Crayfish Feast

Kräftskiva

Sweden's late summer crayfish party, brought to your table in the Bay Area. We bring the whole format, from the dill brined crayfish to the paper hats.

Get on the List

The Tradition

Loud, Messy, and Eaten With Your Hands.

Every late summer, traditionally in August, Swedes gather outdoors for the kräftskiva. Crayfish are boiled in salted water heavy with crown dill, chilled overnight, and eaten cold with your hands. Paper moon lanterns hang overhead. Bibs and party hats go on at every seat. Someone starts a song before every round of snaps.

In Finland the same party is called rapujuhlat. Wherever you grew up with it, it is the opposite of a plated dinner. That is the point.

Two Ways to Throw One

Drop Off or Full Party.

Drop Off
You host · we prep
  • Everything arrives cold and ready to eat
  • The full spread and table kit
  • Printed instructions, because the technique needs explaining
  • You set the table and we are out of your way
Private Party
We run it · you eat
  • Moon lanterns, candles and decor, set up for you
  • An expanded spread with pickled herring and a bigger cheese board
  • An on site crew for service and full cleanup
  • A guide to the eating order and the songs, if you want one

Who It's For

Bring Your People.

  • Nordic companies and teams in the Bay Area marking the season together
  • Swedish, Finnish and Nordic clubs and community groups
  • Offices that want a team party people will still talk about in winter
  • Anyone who has been to one in Sweden and misses it, or never has and should
