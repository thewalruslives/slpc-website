Crew Call

Enter the crew code Aaron gave you.

Tell Aaron which boils you can work.

That is the whole job. Every event on the schedule needs an answer from you — yes or no. It takes a few seconds each and it is how the crew for each boil gets set.

Answer even when you can’t make it.

This is the important one. If you tap Can’t make it, Aaron knows you saw the date and ruled it out. If you leave it blank he has no idea whether you are unavailable or simply haven’t looked — and he ends up chasing you. A no is a useful answer. Silence isn’t.

Doing it

  1. Pick your name

    Top right of any page, next to I am. Your phone remembers it, so you only do this once.

  2. Open the schedule

    The Schedule tab lists every upcoming boil with its support hours — the times you would actually be working. The gold bar shows the boil itself sitting inside that window.

  3. Answer each one

    Under every event: Yes, I can work, Maybe, or Can’t make it. Tap the same button again to undo it. Your answer saves straight away — there is no submit button.

  4. Say if you can deliver

    Tap I can deliver when you can bring the crawfish or gear to the site. It is separate from working the shift.

  5. Leave a note if it needs one

    “Can only stay until 7”, “can bring the big pot”, “need a ride from Dublin”. Aaron reads these on the event, so put anything he’d otherwise have to text you about.

Saying yes isn’t the same as being booked

On the crew

Aaron has picked you. This is the one that counts — green means you are working it. It also appears under My shifts.

The other tabs

My shifts
Your next shift and how far off it is, everything you are on, the hours you have booked, and anything still waiting on your answer.
Calendar
The month at a glance. Gold means you are on it, green means the crew is covered, rust means it still needs people. Tap a day for the detail.
Schedule → Needs crew
The boils still short of people. If you can pick one up, start here.
Changed your mind?

Just tap a different answer. Nothing is locked, and Aaron sees the change. If you are already on the crew and something has come up, change your answer and tell him — don’t rely on the app to chase it.
